WORKING PROCESS

Our Simple & Effective Process

At Classic Fumigation, we follow a systematic, safe and eco-friendly pest control process to ensure long-lasting protection for your home and business.

Inspection

Our experts carefully inspect your property to identify pest activity, affected areas and possible entry points.

Planning

We prepare a customized treatment plan based on the type of pest infestation and the property's requirements.

Treatment

Safe and eco-friendly chemicals are applied using modern equipment to eliminate pests effectively.

Monitoring

Our team monitors the treated area and provides follow-up visits to ensure complete pest control.

Completion

Final inspection is performed and we ensure your property remains safe, hygienic and pest-free.
